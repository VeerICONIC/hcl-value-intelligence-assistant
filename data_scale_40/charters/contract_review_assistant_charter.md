# Initiative Charter: Contract Review Assistant (INIT-004)

## Overview

The Contract Review Assistant highlights non-standard clauses, missing terms and risky obligations in supplier and customer contracts before legal review.
Business unit: Legal. Initiative type: Copilot.

## Objectives

- Speed up first-pass contract review
- Flag deviations from the Group's standard clause library
- Let lawyers focus on negotiation rather than reading

## Scope

In scope: Supplier and customer contracts in English using standard templates.
Out of scope: Litigation documents, employment contracts and contracts in other languages.

## Success Measures

- Legal and procurement teams use the assistant for most new contracts
- Shorter turnaround from contract receipt to first review
- No missed critical clauses in sampled reviews

## Benefits Plan

Benefits are external counsel fees avoided and faster contract cycle times. Realised benefit is reported by the Legal Operations manager.

## Risks and Mitigations

- Risk: Low adoption by senior lawyers who prefer manual review Mitigation: Run clause-level accuracy demonstrations and add the assistant to the standard intake workflow.
- Risk: Assistant misses jurisdiction-specific terms Mitigation: Lawyers retain sign-off; flagged gaps feed back into the clause library.

- Product owner: Aisha Bello, Legal Operations Manager
- Technical lead: Lukas Novak, AI Platform Team

## Timeline

- Phase 1: Pilot with procurement contracts
- Phase 2: Rollout to the full legal team
- Phase 3: Expansion to customer contracts
