# Initiative Charter: Audit Bot (INIT-033)

## Overview

The Audit Bot supports the Audit function at Nimbus Group as a conversational bot.

## Objectives

- Reduce manual effort in audit processes
- Give audit teams faster, consistent answers
- Improve decision quality with traceable outputs

## Scope

In scope: core audit workflows in the main business units.
Out of scope: regulated decisions without human approval.

## Success Measures

- Regular weekly use by the target user group
- Measurable time saved against the pre-launch baseline

## Benefits Plan

Benefits are tracked by the Audit operations lead and reported to the AI portfolio office.

## Stakeholders

- Executive sponsor: Head of Audit
- Product owner: Audit Operations Lead

## Timeline

- Phase 1: Pilot
- Phase 2: Rollout
- Phase 3: Optimisation
