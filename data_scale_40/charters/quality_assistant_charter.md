# Initiative Charter: Quality Assistant (INIT-010)

## Overview

The Quality Assistant supports the Quality function at Nimbus Group as a copilot.

## Objectives

- Reduce manual effort in quality processes
- Give quality teams faster, consistent answers
- Improve decision quality with traceable outputs

## Scope

In scope: core quality workflows in the main business units.
Out of scope: regulated decisions without human approval.

## Success Measures

- Regular weekly use by the target user group
- Measurable time saved against the pre-launch baseline

## Benefits Plan

Benefits are tracked by the Quality operations lead and reported to the AI portfolio office.

## Risks and Mitigations

- Risk: Low adoption by quality staff. Mitigation: Training and workflow integration.
- Risk: Data quality issues in source systems. Mitigation: Data owner reviews before each release.

## Stakeholders

- Executive sponsor: Head of Quality
- Product owner: Quality Operations Lead

## Timeline

- Phase 1: Pilot
- Phase 2: Rollout
- Phase 3: Optimisation
