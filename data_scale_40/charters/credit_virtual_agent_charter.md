# Initiative Charter: Credit Virtual Agent (INIT-040)

## Overview

The Credit Virtual Agent supports the Credit function at Nimbus Group as a conversational bot.

## Objectives

- Reduce manual effort in credit processes
- Give credit teams faster, consistent answers
- Improve decision quality with traceable outputs

## Scope

In scope: core credit workflows in the main business units.
Out of scope: regulated decisions without human approval.

## Success Measures

- Regular weekly use by the target user group
- Measurable time saved against the pre-launch baseline

## Benefits Plan

Benefits are tracked by the Credit operations lead and reported to the AI portfolio office.

## Risks and Mitigations

- Risk: Low adoption by credit staff. Mitigation: Training and workflow integration.
- Risk: Data quality issues in source systems. Mitigation: Data owner reviews before each release.

## Stakeholders

- Executive sponsor: Head of Credit
- Product owner: Credit Operations Lead

## Timeline

- Phase 1: Pilot
- Phase 2: Rollout
- Phase 3: Optimisation
