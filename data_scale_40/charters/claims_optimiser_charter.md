# Initiative Charter: Claims Optimiser (INIT-037)

## Overview

The Claims Optimiser supports the Claims function at Nimbus Group as a predictive model.

## Objectives

- Reduce manual effort in claims processes
- Give claims teams faster, consistent answers
- Improve decision quality with traceable outputs

## Scope

In scope: core claims workflows in the main business units.
Out of scope: regulated decisions without human approval.

## Success Measures

- Regular weekly use by the target user group
- Measurable time saved against the pre-launch baseline

## Benefits Plan

Benefits are tracked by the Claims operations lead and reported to the AI portfolio office.

## Risks and Mitigations

- Risk: Low adoption by claims staff. Mitigation: Training and workflow integration.
- Risk: Data quality issues in source systems. Mitigation: Data owner reviews before each release.

## Stakeholders

- Executive sponsor: Head of Claims
- Product owner: Claims Operations Lead

## Timeline

- First phase: Pilot
- Second phase: Rollout
- Third phase: Optimisation
