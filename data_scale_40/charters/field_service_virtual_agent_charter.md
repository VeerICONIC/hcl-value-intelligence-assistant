# Initiative Charter: Field Service Virtual Agent (INIT-019)

## Overview

The Field Service Virtual Agent supports the Field Service function at Nimbus Group as a conversational bot.

## Objectives

- Reduce manual effort in field service processes
- Give field service teams faster, consistent answers
- Improve decision quality with traceable outputs

## Scope

In scope: core field service workflows in the main business units.
Out of scope: regulated decisions without human approval.

## Success Measures

- Regular weekly use by the target user group
- Measurable time saved against the pre-launch baseline

## Benefits Plan

Benefits are tracked by the Field Service operations lead and reported to the AI portfolio office.

## Risks and Mitigations

- Risk: Low adoption by field service staff. Mitigation: Training and workflow integration.
- Risk: Data quality issues in source systems. Mitigation: Data owner reviews before each release.

## Stakeholders

- Executive sponsor: Head of Field Service
- Product owner: Field Service Operations Lead

## Timeline

- Phase 1: Pilot
- Phase 2: Rollout
- Phase 3: Optimisation
