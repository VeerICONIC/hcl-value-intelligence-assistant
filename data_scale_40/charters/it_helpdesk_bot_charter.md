# Initiative Charter: IT Helpdesk Bot (INIT-002)

## Overview

The IT Helpdesk Bot resolves common IT requests such as password resets, VPN access and software installation through a conversational interface in the employee portal.
Business unit: IT Operations. Initiative type: Conversational bot.

## Objectives

- Deflect routine tickets from the service desk
- Shorten time to resolution for common requests
- Provide self-service support outside office hours

## Scope

In scope: Password resets, account unlocks, VPN and Wi-Fi troubleshooting, standard software requests.
Out of scope: Hardware repairs, security incidents and changes to production systems.

## Success Measures

- High weekly usage across the employee base
- Lower ticket volume for covered request types
- Resolution without agent hand-off for most covered requests

## Benefits Plan

Benefits are measured as service-desk agent hours avoided and reduced outsourcing spend. Costs are reported monthly by the IT finance partner.

## Risks and Mitigations

- Risk: Bot answers drift from current IT procedures Mitigation: Knowledge articles are versioned and re-synced weekly.
- Risk: Users bypass the bot for convenience Mitigation: Portal routes covered request types to the bot first.

## Stakeholders

- Executive sponsor: Arjun Mehta, Chief Information Officer
- Product owner: Sara Lindqvist, Service Desk Manager
- Technical lead: Tom Becker, Automation Engineering

## Timeline

- First phase: Launch for password and access requests
- Second phase: Extension to software requests
- Third phase: Integration with the asset management system
