# Initiative Charter: HR Policy Copilot (INIT-001)

## Overview

The HR Policy Copilot gives employees instant answers to questions about leave, benefits, travel and conduct policies, drawing only on the approved HR policy library.
Business unit: Human Resources. Initiative type: Copilot.

## Objectives

- Reduce routine policy questions sent to HR business partners
- Give employees consistent, policy-accurate answers at any time
- Free HR staff for complex employee cases

## Scope

In scope: All permanent employees in the Group's head office and regional offices; policies in the approved HR library.
Out of scope: Payroll queries, individual employment disputes and performance reviews.

## Success Measures

- Majority of eligible employees use the copilot weekly
- Fewer repetitive tickets reach HR business partners
- Employee satisfaction with policy answers improves

## Benefits Plan

Benefits come from HR time saved on repetitive questions and faster resolution of employee requests. Benefits are tracked monthly by the HR Operations lead against the plan agreed at approval.

## Risks and Mitigations

- Risk: Outdated policy content Mitigation: Policy owners review the library monthly; superseded documents are removed.
- Risk: Over-reliance on copilot answers for sensitive cases Mitigation: The copilot redirects grievance and medical questions to a human HR partner.

- Product owner: Daniel Okafor, HR Operations Lead
- Technical lead: Mei Lin, AI Platform Team

## Timeline

- Phase 1: Pilot with two departments
- Phase 2: Group-wide rollout
- Phase 3: Quarterly content and accuracy review
