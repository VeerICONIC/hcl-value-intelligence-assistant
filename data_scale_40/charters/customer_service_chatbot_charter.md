# Initiative Charter: Customer Service Chatbot (INIT-005)

## Overview

The Customer Service Chatbot answers customer questions about orders, deliveries and returns on the website and mobile app, handing complex cases to human agents.
Business unit: Customer Operations. Initiative type: Conversational bot.

## Objectives

- Handle a large share of routine customer contacts automatically
- Reduce average handling time in the contact centre
- Offer support around the clock

## Scope

In scope: Order status, delivery tracking, returns and refunds policy questions.
Out of scope: Complaints escalation, payment disputes and account security issues.

## Success Measures

- High weekly usage by customers contacting support
- Lower contact-centre volume for covered topics
- Customer satisfaction maintained or improved

## Benefits Plan

Benefits are contact-centre agent costs avoided. Costs include platform licences, integration with the order system and ongoing conversation design. Finance reports benefits in thousands of dollars.

## Risks and Mitigations

- Risk: Integration costs with the legacy order system exceed plan Mitigation: Phase integrations and review spend monthly with Finance.
- Risk: Customers abandon the bot and call the contact centre Mitigation: Improve hand-off design and monitor containment weekly.
