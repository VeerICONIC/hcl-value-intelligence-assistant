# Initiative Charter: Marketing Content Copilot (INIT-007)

## Overview

The Marketing Content Copilot drafts campaign copy, product descriptions and social posts in the Group's brand voice for marketers to edit and approve.
Business unit: Marketing. Initiative type: Copilot.

## Objectives

- Shorten time to produce first drafts of campaign content
- Keep copy consistent with brand guidelines
- Increase the volume of localised content

## Scope

In scope: Marketing team members in brand, product and regional marketing.
Out of scope: Legal disclaimers, regulated product claims and press releases.

## Success Measures

- Most marketers use the copilot weekly
- Faster draft turnaround for campaigns
- Brand compliance maintained in sampled content

## Benefits Plan

Benefits are agency copywriting spend avoided and marketer time saved. The Marketing Operations lead reports benefit quarterly.

## Risks and Mitigations

- Risk: Generated copy breaches brand or regulatory guidelines Mitigation: All output requires marketer approval; regulated claims are blocked.
- Risk: Usage reporting gaps from the analytics tool Mitigation: Reconcile usage logs weekly with the platform vendor.

## Stakeholders

- Executive sponsor: Sophie Laurent, Chief Marketing Officer
- Product owner: Carlos Mendes, Marketing Operations Lead
- Technical lead: Mei Lin, AI Platform Team

## Timeline

- Phase 1: Pilot with brand team
- Phase 2: Rollout to regional marketing
- Phase 3: Localisation workflows
